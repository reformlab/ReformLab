### 2. Non-Deterministic Expand/Scroll/Highlight Spec

**Impact:** High risk of flaky tests and inconsistent UX  
**Source:** AC3, AC6, click-to-preview implementation pattern

**Problem:** Timing, target identity, and highlight lifecycle are underspecified; selectors may collide across cards.

**Recommended Fix:** Add deterministic contract:
- Chip click must expand same card only.
- Scroll target must be unique (`data-group-key="${instanceId}:${groupId}"`).

- Tests must assert exactly one highlighted group at a time.
