**Benefit:** Prevents subtle test failures where repeated adapter instantiation re-registers already-registered variables.

**Suggested Addition:**

> **Extension loading pattern:** Call `add_variable()` on the freshly-instantiated TBS object inside `_get_tax_benefit_system()` immediately after `tbs_class()`.
