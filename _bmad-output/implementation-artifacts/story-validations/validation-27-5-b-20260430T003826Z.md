### 1. Debounce/Clear Race Can Violate AC #3/#4/#5

**Impact:** User can load/save/clear and still see an old draft restored later.  
**Source:** Task 2.4 + Task 4.1–4.4

**Problem:**  
The story requires debounced autosave and explicit draft clear, but does not require cancellation of in-flight timers or suppression of post-clear autosave writes.

**Recommended Fix:**
